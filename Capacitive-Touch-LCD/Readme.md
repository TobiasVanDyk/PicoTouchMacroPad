# Pico Capacitive Touch Macropad LCD1 and LCD2

[**LCD1**](LCD1) is the [**Waveshare RP2350B-A4 FT6336 Capacitive Touch ST7796 LCD 3.5 480x320 with RTC and SDCard module**](https://www.waveshare.com/RP2350-Touch-LCD-3.5.htm), and is the device of choice for the Touch Macropad as it costs little more than the similar sized resistive touch version. It requires only a few external libraries and no calibration before use, and is also an IPS panel with good visibilty when used lying flat. It has an advanced function power switch for the LCD and expansion socket 3v3 power, and audio and sensor modules. 

[**LCD2**](LCD2) is the [**Pico 1 RP2040**](https://www.raspberrypi.org/products/raspberry-pi-pico/) used with a [**DFRobot Fermion Display DFR0669**](https://www.dfrobot.com/product-2107.html). It is a 3.5-inch IPS display with a resolution of 480x320 pixels and capacitive touch using the GT911 touch chip. The module uses the ILI9488 driver chip and communicates via a 4-wire SPI interface. The board includes an onboard MicroSD card slot for storage and operates on a voltage of 3.3V to 5.5V. The Pico 1 handles the shared SDCard and LCD SPI driver well, and allows it to operate at the maximum speed. The Pico 2 was tried but it was the problematic A2 stepping version, and therefore required the SPI read and write speed to be considerably reduced.
